Shape routes chat through cloud models. Sign in is required. **Auto** is included on every plan. Paid plans unlock premium models billed in **credits**.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Credits / mo | Models |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** | $0 | Auto allowance only | Auto |
| **Plus** | $15 | 450 | Auto + all premium models |
| **Pro** | $50 | 1,500 | Auto + all premium models |
| **Max** | $150 | 4,500 | Auto + all premium models |

Higher-tier models use more credits per turn, so the same pool lasts longer on balanced models than on flagship ones. Check usage from the avatar menu or the [usage dashboard](/dashboard/settings/usage).

## Auto

**Auto** uses Shape's included allowance (not premium credits). It routes to a fast backend model for everyday work. Free accounts can use Auto only; Plus and above keep Auto plus the full premium catalog.

## Premium models

When you pick a specific model, usage is charged from your monthly credit pool. Rates below are **credits per million tokens**. Flagship models drain the pool faster.

## Choosing a model

| Goal | Start with |
| --- | --- |
| Everyday edits and questions | **Auto** |
| Hard multi-file refactors | **Claude Opus 5** or **GPT-5.6 Sol** |
| Strong coding, lower cost than Opus | **Claude Sonnet 5** or **Sonnet 4.6** |
| Huge context / many files | **Gemini 3.1 Pro** |
| Stretch credits | **DeepSeek V3.2** or **Auto** |

## Tips

- Prefer **Ask** mode when you do not need edits.
- Start with **Auto** before spending credits on flagship models.
- Turn models on or off in **Settings → Agents**, and set a default for new chats.
- When you hit the credit limit, upgrade or wait for the next monthly reset.

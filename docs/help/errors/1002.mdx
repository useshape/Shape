## What it means

Your account session expired, was revoked, or you signed out elsewhere.

## What to do

1. Sign in again from the app or [login](/login).
2. If you changed your password or enabled 2FA, complete the new challenge.
3. If it keeps happening, revoke sessions in the dashboard and sign in once.

## What it means

Payment for your plan failed, or the subscription is past due. Local editing still works. Premium AI is paused until billing is current.

## What to do

1. Open [Billing](/dashboard/settings/billing).
2. Choose **Manage billing** and update your card.
3. Pay any open invoice in the portal, or wait for Stripe to retry.

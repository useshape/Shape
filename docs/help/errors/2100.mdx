## What it means

Too many AI requests were sent in a short time. The provider or Shape Cloud asked you to wait.

## What to do

1. Wait a short time, then send the message again.
2. Avoid rapidly retrying the same request.
3. If this happens often on a paid plan, check [billing](/dashboard/settings/billing) and contact support.

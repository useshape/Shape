**Error 2001: Usage or credits**

You have reached a usage or credit limit for this request.

## What to try

1. Check your plan and remaining credits on the [billing page](/dashboard/settings/billing).
2. Use Auto if you are on the free tier, or wait for monthly Auto allowance to reset.
3. Upgrade your plan if you need more premium model capacity.

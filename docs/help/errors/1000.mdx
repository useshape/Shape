**Error 1000: Auth required**

You need to be signed in to your Shape account for this action.

## What to try

1. Sign in from Settings → Account, or from the titlebar account menu.
2. If sign-in fails, complete the browser flow and return to the app.
3. Sign out and sign in again if your session expired.

## Shape will not open on Windows

Use Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). Download again from [Download](/download) or [GitHub releases](https://github.com/useshape/Shape/releases).

## Sign-in fails in the desktop app

1. Finish sign-in in the browser when prompted.
2. Sign out and sign in again from the avatar menu.

See [Accounts](/docs/introduction/accounts).

## AI says usage is not allowed

Check your plan and credits on [Billing](/dashboard/settings/billing). Free tier cannot use all paid models.

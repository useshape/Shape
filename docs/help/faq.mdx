## Is Shape free?

Yes. Free includes Auto model access. Paid plans add more models and credits. See [Pricing](/pricing).

## Where is my data stored?

Editing is local. Cloud AI runs when you are signed in. See [Privacy](/privacy).

## How do credits work?

Paid models use credits. Check usage on the [dashboard](/dashboard/settings/usage) or from the account menu in the app.

## Can I use Shape offline?

Local editing works offline. AI needs a network connection and a Shape account.

## What license is Shape under?

[BUSL-1.1](https://github.com/useshape/Shape/blob/main/LICENSE).

## Can I inspect a live page?

Yes. Open the [Browser](/docs/editor/visual-design) tab, load your local URL, turn on design mode, and **Apply** changes to source.

## Can I review pull requests in Shape?

Yes. Open [Git Manager](/docs/git/git-manager) (**Window → Git Manager**) after [GitHub sign-in](/docs/git/github-sign-in).

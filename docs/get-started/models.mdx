Auto is included on every plan. A named model spends credits. Unused credits do not carry over. Usage is on the [usage page](/dashboard/settings/usage).

| Plan | Price | Credits / month | Models |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | Auto only | Auto |
| Plus | $15 | 450 | Auto and every model below |
| Pro | $50 | 1,500 | Auto and every model below |
| Max | $150 | 4,500 | Auto and every model below |

Rates are credits per million tokens (provider USD × 1.4 margin ÷ $0.02 per credit).
